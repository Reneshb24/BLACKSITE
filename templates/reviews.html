REVIEW INTELLIGENCE

Review records describe how authorization requests were evaluated. A review is evidence of an authorization decision, not authorization by itself. Cross-reference the reviewer, principal, policy revision and later operation history.
INVESTIGATION RULE
An APPROVED review does not automatically prove that a later delegation or capability was valid. Follow the complete authorization chain.
