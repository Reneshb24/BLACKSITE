DELEGATION INTELLIGENCE

Delegations record the transfer of authority between a principal and a delegate. Scope, policy revision, lifecycle state and identity relationships must be evaluated together.
INVESTIGATION RULE
An ACTIVE delegation is not sufficient evidence by itself. Determine whether its scope, policy revision, principal, delegate and later authorization operation agree.
